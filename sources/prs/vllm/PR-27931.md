## Purpose

Optimize rms_norm kernel:
* Do vectorized load and store using `vec_n_t<scalar_t, VEC_SIZE>`.
* Since input, weight, output tensors are the same dtype, and all of their last dimension are hidden_size, we can get the largest safe vec_size by calculating the number that can divide hidden_size and fits in 16 bytes, to avoid misalignment.
* For large num_tokens (>=256), use smaller blocks to increase SM concurrency.
