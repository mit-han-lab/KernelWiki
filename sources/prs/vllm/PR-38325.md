## Purpose
This PR adds swap AB kernel support for the SM120 (Blackwell) blockwise FP8 scaled GEMM.

For decode-phase workloads and small-batch inference, the M dimension of the GEMM is very small. Without swap AB, the tile partitioning along M is highly inefficient — most threads within a CTA tile are idle. By transposing the problem as `D = (B^T @ A^T)^T`, the small dimension is moved to N, enabling better tile coverage and higher SM occupancy.
