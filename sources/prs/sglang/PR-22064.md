## Motivation

Two bugs in the diffusion FLUX.2-dev-NVFP4 path:

1. `ModelOptFp4LinearMethod.process_weights_after_loading()` was missing the blockwise interleave (swizzle) required by CUTLASS TMA kernels. The CUTLASS fallback path silently produced wrong results, caused ~5% cosine-similarity drop vs cuDNN.
2. `KernelConfigDefault` (cluster `4×4`) left ~25% of the last M-wave idle for FLUX.2 at 1024px (M≈4352, N=3072).

Also removes the `comfy-kitchen` third-party dependency.

## Modifications

- **`nvfp4_scaled_mm_kernels.cuh`**: Add `KernelConfigLargeM` (cluster `1×4`) for M > 1024; narrow `KernelConfigDefault` to M ∈ (256, 1024] with cluster `2×4`.
- **`modelopt_quant.py`**: Add missing `reshape + permute` swizzle in `process_weights_after_loading`; remove `ComfyUIFp4LinearMethod` and `_get_comfy_kitchen_cuda_backend`.
- **`cuda.py`**: Remove comfy-kitchen platform detection methods.

## Speed Tests and Profiling

No regression. Denoise 50 steps on FLUX.2-dev-NVFP4 on B200:
- cuDNN: ~0.326 s/step
- CUTLASS: ~0.292 s/step
