## Motivation
Until now, the CUTLASS-based nvfp4 block-scaled GEMM kernel used a uniform performance config for all MNKs. This config performed well when MNKs were large, but it resulted in redundant computation when M was small. Therefore, we have optimized this for scenarios with small M. In this PR, we would like to highlight several sgl-kernel specific optimizations:

- Fine-tuned ClusterShape: ClusterShape affects the direction of TMA Multicast.
