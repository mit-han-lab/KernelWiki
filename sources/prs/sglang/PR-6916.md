## Motivation

A fusion kernel that improves the overall CUTLASS MOE layer perf for B200.
Fuses this single line:
`(c2[c_map].view(m, topk, k) * topk_weights.view(m, topk, 1).to(out_dtype)).sum(dim=1)`
