## Motivation

Refer to the implementation at [here](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/main/python/sglang/jit_kernel/csrc/elementwise/fused_add_rmsnorm.cuh) , which implements a cross-head QK Norm kernel commonly used in diffusion models such as [Wan](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/main/python/sglang/multimodal_gen/runtime/models/dits/wanvideo.py#L336-L338), LTX2(https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/blob/main/python/sglang/multimodal_gen/runtime/models/dits/ltx_2.py#L489-L502) video model.

Currently, SGLang Diffusion employs a standard PyTorch implementation. Based on the micro-benchmark below, applying this optimization should yield benefits for Diffusion model applications.
