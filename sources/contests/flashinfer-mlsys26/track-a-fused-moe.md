# Track A: Fused MoE

## Official scope

The official MLSys 2026 FlashInfer AI Kernel Generation Contest page describes Track A as fused Mixture-of-Experts kernels with FP8 support. Submissions targeted NVIDIA Blackwell B200 GPUs and could be expert-crafted with agent assistance or fully agent-generated.

## Official results

The official page reports separate top-three lists for the two approaches:
